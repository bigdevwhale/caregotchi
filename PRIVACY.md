# Privacy

Caregotchi has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your marks, medicine, troubles, pets and settings stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on.

Only one thing goes over the network: **the weather and the air pressure for the window.** The coordinates of the town you chose (or the one guessed from your time zone) are sent to Open-Meteo, or to MET Norway when Open-Meteo can't be reached. Nothing else goes with them.

Notifications are used for the pet's reminders (medicine, meals, water, bedtime) and for «Feeling better?» after a bad spell. The doctor's PDF and the backup file leave the phone only when you send or save them yourself.

The app is a diary, not medical advice.
